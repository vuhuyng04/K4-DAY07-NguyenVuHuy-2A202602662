# Hours and access

## Hours

Opening hours from July to August:

- Monday to Friday: 8:45 am – 5:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: Closed (except for VinUni staff working Saturdays)
- 24/7 Learning Space: Remains open and accessible at all times.

Please note: during this period the circulation desk will not be operational, but information services will still be available upon request via email.

Opening hours from September:

- Monday to Friday: 8:45 am – 9:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: 9:00 am – 5:00 pm
- The 24/7 learning space will always remain open.

## Access

Students, faculty, staff, alumni, and members of the University can access the university library using their current VinUni ID card.

Staff from other Vingroup's Profits and Losts (P&Ls) or VinUni's counterparts who wish to access library materials need to apply for a library card.

24/7 learning space: access to the 24/7 Learning space after the Library closes is restricted to current VinUni students, faculty, and staff only.

## Location

Building A, VinUniversity. Vinhomes Ocean Park, Gia Lam District, Hanoi.
