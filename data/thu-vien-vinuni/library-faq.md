# Library FAQ

## 1. What are Library operating hours?

Opening hours from July to August, 2024: Monday to Friday: 8:45 am – 5:00 pm. Saturday and Sunday: Closed (except for working Saturdays). The 24/7 learning space will always remain open.

Opening hours for other months, 2024: Monday to Friday: 8:45 am – 9:00 pm. Saturday and Sunday: 9:00 am – 5:00 pm. The 24/7 learning space will always remain open.

Please note: during July and August, the library will be partially open, with only the second floor accessible. The first floor will be closed. The circulation desk will not be operational, but information services will still be available upon request.

## 2. When in need of information support or clarification, how could I contact the library?

Meet us: Circulation desk, 1st floor, building A, VinUniversity. Phone: +84-2471089779, ext: 9007. Chat with us via website: https://library.vinuni.edu.vn.

Please refer to the library policy on the library's website.

No, you do not. You can borrow books from library for free.

You can use the self-check machine on either the 1st floor or the 2nd floor to borrow or return print books. The 24/7 book return machine at the library main entrance is just for checking in books only.

## 7. What is the penalty for returning books late?

The library charges an overdue fine for late payment of the borrowed items from the due date or the expiration of the recall to the actual date of return and the date of notification of lost items from patrons. In addition, overdue fines always apply including holidays, vacations, and days off. Fines are collected at the time incurred.

- Normal material: 20,000 VND/day overdue/document.
- Course-specific material: 20,000 VND/hour overdue/document.
- Equipment: 20,000 VND/hour overdue/equipment.

Note: Failure to make a payment on time does not excuse patrons from the responsibility of returning or renewing items. Patrons must pay both an overdue fine and an additional fee equivalent to the cover price of borrowed items if they have not been returned 30 days after the due date.

## 8. What should I do if I lose or damage a library book?

Lost or damaged items should be reported to the library in a timely manner. Patrons will be charged fines for overdue materials/equipment or material/equipment repair and replacement fees. These fines and fees are regulated in VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.

Minor damage (slightly damaged and repairable): book covers with markings on the inside or outside, or portions ripped off; book pages with markings on less than 10 pages, medium creases, tears, stains, or liquid damage; equipment damage to be determined by IT/Operation department.

Major damage or loss: book cover is damaged or missing; pages are stained, missing, illegible or warped; book is written upon or marked throughout; other conditions that render the book unusable, such as a strong odor; an audio-visual item is rendered unable to be read by devices; equipment is seriously broken and unrepairable; books/equipment/CD-DVDs are reported lost.

## 9. What should I do if I discover that a borrowed document is torn, missing pages, or marked?

If you discover that a borrowed document is torn, missing pages, or marked, you must immediately report it to the library staff to avoid being held responsible for the missing pages. Provide them with specific details about the damage, such as the extent of tearing, the number of missing pages, or the nature of the markings.

## 10. Where can I see the return date or overdue book return date?

To view information about borrowing books, due dates, or overdue status, you can visit the library website and select the "My Library Account" section. Your login credentials will be your VinUni account.

## 11. After returning the book to the library, why do I still see it listed as overdue in my account?

First, please be sure that you returned the books at self-check machines or 24/7 return station in library before bringing the books to the bookshelves. Second, check your email to see if there is any confirmation email from library about your return action. Finally, contact library staff directly at the Information Desk.

## 12. How to find books at the library?

Start your search at the VinUni library homepage. Log in with your valid VinUni account. Enter your keywords into the search box then select library catalog (default search is 'Everything'). You can also search with keywords and then apply a filter to your results. Filters are shown to the left of your search results.

## 13. How to renew a borrowed print book?

Go to library home page and log in with your VinUni ID. Click RENEW ALL if you wish to renew all the borrowed books, or click LOAN and RENEW individual books. Tip: do the renewal one day just before the original due date to achieve the longest renewed due date.

## 14. Can I photocopy documents in the library?

Yes, you can. The library provides photocopy and document scanning services. Readers are allowed to photocopy and scan articles, book pages, theses, dissertations, projects, scientific research topics, and conference materials. Follow the guidance next to the photocopying counters at the 1st floor of the library. Please be aware of the Copyright Law before copying a library book.

## 15. Can I borrow someone else's library card to access the library?

No, you cannot. Sharing your VinUni library ID or library card with others is strictly prohibited.

## 16. How are books arranged on the shelves?

VinUni Library uses the Library of Congress Classification (LCC) system to classify and arrange books on the shelves. The call number starts with letters representing the subject of the book, followed by numbers indicating the specific content of the book. This call number is printed and attached to the spine of the book and is used to arrange books on the shelves, in left-to-right and top-to-bottom order.

## 19. I borrowed the book through the self-check machine, but the alarm still rang from the RFID gate, what should I do?

Please bring the book to Circulation desk at the 1st floor, library staff will support you.

## 20. What should I do if I can't print?

First, be sure that you follow all the steps in the guidance. Then check the error message displaying on the printer: HELP/SPOOLING error — check if the printer tray is securely closed and the formatting is correct (Tray 1, A4 paper). Unable to access the computer for printing — log in to the student Wi-Fi network and sign in to your Outlook email account. Paper jam error — open all paper trays to deal with any jammed paper, then close the paper tray. If the printer is still not working, contact the IT team: Hotline IT +84-2471089779 Ext 9066.

## 21. The scanner is not working, what should I do?

Check if the scanner switch is turned on or restart the device. If the scanner is still not working, contact the IT team: Hotline IT +84-2471089779 Ext 9066.

## 22. Can I borrow library's equipment for a long-time event outside of the library building?

No, you cannot. The library's equipment can be used within 1 working day in the library only. Exception: for some external events, you need confirmation from the event sponsor, who should contact the library manager for approval.
